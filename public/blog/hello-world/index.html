Hello World — Starting This Blog

2024-01-15

Every blog starts somewhere. This is mine.

Why I’m Writing

Writing helps me think. When I write something down, I’m forced to be precise about what I actually understand — and more importantly, where the gaps are.

I’ve been in software for a while now, and I’ve accumulated opinions, lessons learned (often the hard way), and observations about the craft. This blog is where I put those to paper.

What to Expect

No fixed schedule, no promises. Just honest writing when I have something worth saying.

Topics will likely include:

  • Software architecture and design decisions
  • Tools and workflows that have changed how I work
  • Lessons from building and shipping things
  • Occasional rabbit holes I go down

If something resonates, I’d love to hear from you.
